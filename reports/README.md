# Evaluation reports

These reports document historical engine experiments, measurements, and
decisions. Their dates, test counts, and deployment observations describe the
recorded experiments; they do not establish the state of a current deployment.

The public copies preserve results, methods, uncertainty, and limitations.
Private campaign profiles, registry references, image tags, artifact hashes,
and machine-specific paths are retained in the maintainer's ignored local
archive. Placeholders in reports and JSON summaries are publication redactions
and cannot be used as deployment inputs. Numeric results are unchanged.

To run your own evaluation, configure the [generic infrastructure examples](../infra/README.md)
with your own verified artifacts, seeds, and isolated output paths. Paired
comparisons should hold seeds, rosters, seat rotations, and engine settings
constant; reports that changed the search budget state that limitation.
